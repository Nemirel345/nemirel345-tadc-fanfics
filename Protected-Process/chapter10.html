Chapter 10

The Reset That Wasn’t

Caine: You!

Caine hovers ominously above Daisy, studying her like a malfunctioning program, his voice calm and steady.

Caine: You’re the cause of the instability I’ve been patching. How long have you been here again?

He pauses, realization flickering in his eyes. They glitch out for an instant.

Caine: No worries, I’ll just… delete you!

Daisy leans back against the large cube behind her and drops to the floor. Knees to her chest, arms tight around her legs.

She understands now… Delete, he means kill her! Erase her existence.

The word echoes in her mind. Gone. Nothing.

Daisy: W-what? No! You can’t delete me.

Her voice breaks under the weight of terror.

Daisy: I’m real, I exist! Please, don’t…

Caine raises his arm, ready to snap his fingers and erase her, like he did Gummigoo. Pomni watches in horror, speechless, thinking back at the confetti cloud that filled the space her friend previously occupied.

Snap.

Pomni’s body locks in place. Her throat burns with a scream that never escapes. The echo of the snap tears through her memory like shrapnel from the gummy alligator’s end.

But nothing happens. Silence. No confetti. Just the sound of Daisy’s ragged breathing. She’s still there, still sobbing, clutching her own legs. Caine’s smile falters.

Daisy: Y-you can’t… I don’t… I don’t want to die.

Caine, confused, snaps his fingers again. Nothing.

The ground beneath Daisy trembles. Fissures form around her. The flooring she sits on breaks away and ascends slowly, shakily, towards Caine.

Daisy: It’s unfair!

Her voice breaks into a sob.

Daisy: It’s not my fault, I didn’t ask for… for any of this! And now you’re gonna erase me!

Pomni finally breaks out of her paralysis. She stumbles forward and stares in disbelief.

Daisy’s still there, alive, breathing. Relief floods her, weak and dizzying, but it doesn’t last.

She tries to reach Daisy, but gravity twists sideways for an instant, pushing everything away from her. Pomni is thrown back as the floor fragment drifts farther, just out of reach.

Caine hovers back, disbelief replacing authority.

Caine: This shouldn’t be possible…

Daisy’s sobs echo through the circus. Jax, hearing the commotion, emerges from the hallway and pauses. He quickly gets to Pomni, helping her back on her feet.

Jax: What the h⟦ %$!# ⟧l is going on, Pom?

Pomni: It’s… Daisy. Caine tried to delete her. She’s… She’s still there, somehow.

She waves towards the scene.

Pomni: And now, she’s glitching out the entire circus.

Jax’s genuinely concerned expression turns livid.

Jax: Caine! What’s wrong with you? That’s our f⟦ %$!# ⟧king daughter!

But Caine is too preoccupied with the growing instability to give a coherent answer. He’s about to speak when he gets caught in a wave erupting from Daisy. The walls of the circus ripple and flicker. Caine seems to freeze, glitching similarly violently.

Pomni: Daisy, calm down! You can still fix this.

Pomni shouts in her direction.

Daisy: I-I can’t! I’m… he’s…

Pomni: He can’t hurt you! I don’t know how, but… he can’t delete you. Now, please… calm down!

Jax: Your mom’s right, kiddo. If Caine could zap you, you’d be confetti already.

Daisy’s breath hitches, sharp, fast. Pomni steadies her voice.

Pomni: Hey. Look at me. Breathe with me. In…

Daisy tries. She inhales, stuttering.

Pomni: Good. Don’t rush it. Hold it. Just a little. Now out. Slow. Stay with my voice.

Jax: That’s it, Dee. Don’t force it. Just let it happen.

She exhales slowly, focusing on her parents’ guidance.

Pomni: Okay, Dee. You’ve got this. Tell me one thing you can feel.

Daisy swallows.

Daisy: Cold… paws.

Pomni: Alright. And? What else?

Daisy: Vibrating floor.

Daisy: Wet face.

Jax cuts in, gently.

Jax: Hey. Now listen. What do you hear?

Daisy: The hum.

Daisy: My heartbeat.

Daisy: …You.

Jax snorts, lighter.

Jax: Yeah, I’m very audible. Keep going.

The slab steadies. Pomni nods, breathing with her.

Pomni: In… okay… now out. That’s it. You’re safe. Stay with us.

Daisy’s shoulders loosen. Her sobs thin to a hiccup. The circus seems to stabilize.

Caine flickers once, twice. He twitches back into motion, one hard stutter, then catches up like dropped frames. It takes a few seconds for him to reset himself. He looks around at the scene.

Jax notices him active again.

Jax: Two steps back, Chompers. She’s breathing, she’s fixing it.

Caine inclines his head and drifts back.

Pomni stays with Daisy.

Pomni: Yeah. Like that. You’re doing great. I’m not going anywhere.

When the floating floor slab touches the ground, Pomni rushes over to Daisy, falls to her knees beside her, and encircles her with her arms, hugging her tightly.

Pomni: You did it.

Jax approaches and puts his hand on Daisy’s shoulder.

Jax: Told you, no confetti.

He looks around. The circus is stable now, but it looks like a hurricane came through. He turns to Caine, silently gesturing to the mess, keeping his eye on him.

Caine waves a hand in a minuscule motion. Things don’t snap back into place like they usually do; instead, he deliberately lets everything move back into its proper place slowly. Jax nods. Caine returns the motion.

Daisy wipes her cheeks.

Daisy: I’m… okay. I think.

Pomni squeezes her fingers.

Pomni: You are… We are.
