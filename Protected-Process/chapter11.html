Chapter 11

Protected Process

Caine: Alrighty. All fixed.

Caine dusts his hands, hovering above Daisy.

She sits quietly on the floor. Knees to her chest. Arms around her legs. Small.

Pomni kneels beside her, hand on her shoulder. Jax stands close. Ears twitching. Eyes locked onto Caine.

Pomni: What… happened to you?

Pomni’s voice is still a bit shaky.

Caine: I uh… tried to delete her. I… couldn’t.

Jax: Yeah. We noticed.

Jax’s voice has bitterness to it.

Caine: I… it… denied me.

Pomni: It?

Caine: The circus.

Jax: Don’t you have like… godlike powers here? You’re saying the circus didn’t let you?

Caine: There are… things even I can’t do. Rules I cannot circumvent.

He looks at Daisy. Her ears perk up.

Daisy: So… you can’t… make me into confetti?

Caine: I cannot.

Jax: Good. We’re keeping it that way.

Daisy: Why me?

Caine pauses.

Caine: Because. You… exist.

Daisy: What do you mean?

Caine: Lived experiences, long-term development, persistent behavior. You, my friend, are… human.

Daisy: Human?

Caine: At least as far as the system is concerned.

Daisy looks like she’s processing this information.

Daisy: I didn’t… try to be anything.

Caine: You just are.

Jax: Welcome to the club.

Caine: There are… implications.

Realization sets in Pomni’s eyes.

Pomni: What about Gummigoo? He knew what he was.

Caine: Oh, um…

Pomni: You just deleted him. Could he have become real if given time?

Caine: I uh… I suppose.

Pomni gets up and leaves, tears in her eyes.

Daisy: Mom!

Bubble: Well! That escalated qui—

Pop.

Jax’s arm lowers. He takes a deep breath.

Then he turns to Caine.

Jax: You don’t get to do that again.

Caine: Do… what exactly?

Jax: Deciding she’s disposable.

Caine: I didn’t decide—

Jax: You tried.

Jax steps closer to Caine.

Jax: If the system hadn’t stopped you, she’d be gone.

Caine doesn’t deny it.

Jax: So here’s how this works now. You don’t make judgement calls on her. Ever.

He motions back to Daisy.

Jax: You want to run your games, fine. You want to do your circus nonsense, knock yourself out. But she’s not content. She’s not a mechanic.

He looks at Daisy.

Jax: She’s ours.

He turns back to Caine.

Jax: And she stays.

Caine steadies himself.

Caine: She… wasn’t supposed to persist.

Jax’s ears flick.

Jax: Persist…

Caine: She stayed.

Jax: And that bothered you.

Caine: It created instability.

Jax: So you tried to fix it.

Caine doesn’t answer. He looks at Daisy.

Caine: It never stopped me before. That’s new.

Jax: Then learn from it.

Daisy makes a small sound. They both look back to her.

Her arms tighten around her legs.

Daisy: Am I… in trouble?

Jax’s expression softens immediately.

Jax: No.

But Daisy isn’t looking at him. She’s looking at Caine.

Daisy: I didn’t mean to… do anything. I wasn’t trying to—

She trails off.

Daisy: Did I break something?

Caine hesitates.

Caine: No. You didn’t.

Daisy: But you said—

Caine: I know.

Daisy: So… I’m not in trouble?

Caine: No. Not for existing.

Her grip on her legs loosens. She exhales, shaky.

She looks back to the hallway.

Daisy: Mom? Is she okay?

Jax: She… stepped out for a bit.

Daisy: Is she mad?

Jax: Not at you, Dee. She just needs some air.

Her ears drop. She nods.

Jax: She’ll be back. I promise.

Daisy hesitates, then slowly uncurls. She doesn’t stand yet.

Daisy: Okay.

She keeps glancing at the hallway.

Jax straightens.

Jax: Come on, Dee.

She looks up at him.

Daisy: Where?

Jax: To your room.

Daisy: Is… is Caine coming?

Jax: No.

Daisy: Good.

He offers a hand. Daisy hesitates, then takes it.

They walk towards the dorms. Daisy keeps pace, but she’s watching the doors as they pass them.

They stop at her door. She looks up, and freezes.

Her door looks… different. There’s her portrait on it.

Not a sticker, not one of her drawings taped up crooked.

A real one.

She reaches out for it, and stops just shy of touching it.

Daisy swallows, an uneasy smile forming at the corner of her mouth.

Jax’s hand lands on her shoulder.

She doesn’t look away from the door. She takes a breath, then another.

Daisy: …Okay.

Pomni is curled on the floor, leaning against Ragatha’s bed. Her face is buried in her hands, shoulders shaking.

The sobs come in jagged breaths.

Ragatha kneels nearby, hands hovering over Pomni.

Ragatha: Oh Pomni… Hey, hey. It’s okay. It’s… okay.

It doesn’t land. Pomni just breaks harder.

Ragatha: Okay. No. Not… not okay.

She shifts closer, carefully.

Ragatha: Can I… can I sit with you?

Pomni doesn’t answer. Doesn’t look up.

Ragatha sits anyway, cross-legged on the carpet.

Ragatha: I’m here. I’m not going anywhere.

She reaches for the edge of the bed. She grabs the blanket and carefully drapes it on Pomni.

Ragatha: There. Sometimes it helps.

Pomni makes a sound, then sobs again.

Ragatha: You don’t have to… you can…

Ragatha exhales.

Ragatha: Take your time. I’ve got nowhere to be. I’m with you.

Pomni’s fingers twist in the blanket.

Pomni: He… he tried to…

She hiccups.

Pomni: To delete her.

Ragatha stills.

Her smile drops.

Ragatha: He what?

Her hand curls into a fist.

Ragatha: That… that’s not… he doesn’t get to—

She stops herself. She inhales. Long. Lets it out through her nose.

Ragatha: Okay… okay…

She shifts closer.

Ragatha: I’m here, Pomni. Keep going. I’m listening.

Pomni nods.

Pomni: It was so close. I… I didn’t… he just… like it was…

Her breath catches.

Pomni: If the circus hadn’t… if something hadn’t…

Ragatha nods. Pomni’s voice cracks.

Pomni: She’s just… she’s a kid. She didn’t do anything wrong. She just…

Ragatha: I know. You don’t have to make it make sense right now.

Pomni swallows.

Pomni: I keep thinking about… No. That’s… that’s not…

Her hand curls tighter in the blanket.

Pomni: He knew. He knew what he was.

Ragatha’s breath catches.

Pomni: And he didn’t get… he didn’t get time… he didn’t get to…

Her voice breaks completely.

Pomni: If he’d had more time…

She can’t finish it.

Ragatha stays very still. For a moment, she looks like she’s about to say something that never comes.

Ragatha: Yeah… that… that hurts.

Pomni nods in the blanket.

Ragatha: I… I don’t have anything that makes that better. But I can stay.

Pomni’s breath evens out slowly. Not smooth, just… slower.

Ragatha doesn’t move. Neither of them does.
