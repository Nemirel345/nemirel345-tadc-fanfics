Chapter 9

Parent Protocols

Daisy: I’m still mad at you.

Pomni’s head snaps up. Jax stops pacing.

Daisy stands at the door to her room, fur still damp around her eyes.

Pomni quickly stands up from sitting on the edge of Daisy’s bed. She crosses the room before she thinks about it.

Pomni: Dee!

She pulls Daisy into a hug. Daisy goes stiff, then sags into it with a shaky breath.

Pomni: You disappeared!

Pomni’s voice cracks.

Pomni: You fell through a mirror, and… and we couldn’t get to you.

Jax: We thought we’d lost you.

Daisy: I said I’m still mad.

Jax: Good. You’re allowed to be. And we’re allowed to be worried about you!

Pomni: You’re allowed to be mad at us. We deserve it.

Daisy looks between them, jaw clenched.

Daisy: You lied to me.

Pomni winces.

Daisy: You knew what I was, and you never told me.

Pomni: We were trying to protect you.

Jax: Which isn’t the same as doing the right thing.

Daisy inhales deeply.

Daisy: I… I talked to Kinger. He told me what NPC means. What Caine does. I know he… made me.

Pomni’s shoulders fall.

Pomni: Okay. Then you know more than we ever managed to say out loud.

Daisy’s ears drop.

Daisy: I’m… still me… I think. I still feel like me. But now I know I’m just a prop. A stupid little prop for one of his stupid adventures. And I keep thinking about it.

Pomni looks away for a moment, then back.

Pomni: Before you, there was… someone else. A friend… from one of Caine’s adventures. He figured out he was… built. Like you.

Daisy: The… gummy guy.

Pomni: Gummigoo. He… started falling apart when he realized what he was. I invited him to stay with us. I thought… it would help.

Daisy: Did it?

Pomni: For a bit. Until the adventure was over and Caine… took him back.

Daisy: And… that was it?

Pomni: No.

Pomni’s mouth twists.

Pomni: A while later, Caine brought him out again. Same body, but no memories of that time. He just… smiled like we never met.

Jax: Caine likes to reuse NPCs.

Daisy goes very still.

Daisy: So… when I showed up… you thought—

Pomni: I thought I was gonna watch that happen again.

Pomni’s voice shakes.

Pomni: So I told myself you weren’t like him. That if I pretended hard enough, that Caine would too. And I couldn’t bear the idea of you looking at me like he did. Like I made it worse by telling him the truth.

Daisy: So you didn’t tell me at all. And instead, I got to find out in the worst way possible.

Pomni nods, eyes wet.

Pomni: I am so, so sorry, Dee.

Jax shifts closer.

Jax: We should have told you sooner. Trusted that we could tell you and you could still be you. That’s on us.

Daisy bites her lip.

Daisy: I know I’m an NPC now. That Caine created me. Does that… change anything? To you? Does it make me less your kid?

Pomni: No! Absolutely not!

Jax: Not even a little.

Pomni: It doesn’t matter to us. It doesn’t change how we feel.

Daisy smiles softly at that. Then drops it.

Daisy: I’m scared. Of Caine. Of my glitching.

Pomni: We’re scared too. Difference is… we’re done being scared behind your back. Whatever happens, we face it together.

Daisy blinks hard.

Daisy: I’m not ready to forgive you yet.

Jax: You don’t have to.

Pomni: You can be mad. You can ask questions. We’ll answer. No more secret meetings about you.

Daisy: I still love you… even with all of this.

Pomni: We never stopped.

Pomni opens her arms. Daisy hesitates, then steps in. Pomni wraps her up and Jax folds around both of them.

Daisy: I’m still mad though.

Jax: Good. Means you’re still you.

Daisy sits in her room, back against her bed. She’s staring at a wooden cube in the center of her room.

Daisy: Okay.

She holds out her hand, fingers spread, like she’s trying to pull it with her mind.

Nothing.

Daisy: Glitch. Come on. Just a little.

She exhales hard.

Daisy: When I don’t want it, everything goes wacky. When I do want it, you’re suddenly very well-behaved. Rude.

She closes her eyes and tries to remember the moments glitching occurred.

The tower, the carnival.

She opens her eyes again.

Daisy: If I’m the one doing it, I should be able to stop doing it. Or… aim. Or something.

She shuffles closer to the cube.

Daisy: Come on. Work with me for once.

She focuses. Inhales. Pushes.

Nothing. No wobble. No flicker.

Daisy: Of course. I’m dangerous, but only on accident. Great.

She sits back against her bed, looks up to the ceiling.

Daisy: Ugh! Why can’t I make it do the thing?

She drops her head forwards and gives the cube a kick, pushing it a few feet.

For a second it wobbles, then stops… then briefly twitches.

Daisy freezes.

Daisy: Okay, that…

She stares at it.

Daisy: Did I…

She gets up and kicks it again, a little harder.

Nothing.

Daisy: Oh, come on!

She frowns, thinking back.

She nudges it gently with her foot.

This time, she lets herself feel them. The emotions she’s been quietly storing. About what she is, about her parents, about Caine.

The cube jitters. For an instant, the colours of the edges seem to split. Then normal again.

Daisy sucks in a breath.

Daisy: I did. I did do it!

Her heart is pounding.

Daisy: Okay, so it’s not the pose. It’s… feelings. Maybe. Kinda.

She leans forwards, hand on the floor. Her face almost touching the cube.

Daisy: If I could just… maybe I can—

A knock at the door.

Daisy jumps.

Pomni: Dee?

Pomni’s voice is muffled across the door.

Pomni: Can we come in?

Daisy’s eyes flick between the cube and the door.

Daisy: Uh… yeah! It’s fine.

The door opens. Pomni and Jax slip in.

They spot the cube on the floor, Daisy on her knees in front of it.

Pomni: Everything okay?

Daisy: I… uh… I did it!

Pomni: Did what?

Daisy: The glitch.

She points at the cube.

Daisy: It went weird. I’ve been trying to see if I could make it happen on purpose, and it did. Just a bit. But it did!

Jax: You’ve been… practicing?

Daisy nods, fast.

Daisy: I know the circus keeps going wonky around me. I thought… if I poke at it, I can figure out how it works.

Pomni: Dee—

Daisy: I was careful! It was just a little. It’s fine now!

She looks between them.

Daisy: This is good… right? If I can make it happen, I can learn how to not make it happen.

Pomni crouches beside her.

Pomni: I’m glad you told us. And I get why you want to understand it. But doing it by yourself is… really scary for us.

Daisy: You’re the one who said no more secrets. I’m not hiding it, I’m telling you.

Jax: And we appreciate that. We’d just like you not to discover Oops, I can drop through the floor while there’s nobody to catch you.

Daisy frowns.

Daisy: So what, I’m not allowed to try unless you’re watching?

Pomni: We don’t want to lock you in a box… but we want to be there. To help. To… pull you out, if it goes wrong.

Jax: If reality’s gonna wobble, just… don’t do it alone.

Daisy looks back at the cube.

She wants to tell them to leave her alone again.

She sighs.

Daisy: Fine… But we actually do it. We don’t just pretend it isn’t a thing.

Pomni: Deal. Tomorrow. We find a quiet spot. Not your room. We’ll… figure out ground rules.

Jax: And maybe no more poking that cube tonight.

Jax taps his foot against a small crate in a quiet part of the tent.

Jax: Test subject?

Daisy nods.

Jax: Okay. Ground rules first. If anything feels too weird, you stop. If we say stop, you stop. No pushing through just to prove a point.

Daisy rolls her eyes.

Daisy: Got it. Safety lecture, check.

Pomni: We’re serious, Dee.

Jax points at the crate.

Jax: Alright, Gremlin. See what you can do to that…

He looks at Pomni.

Jax: …Gently. No turn the world inside-out.

Daisy: Okay.

She steps closer, holds out her hand.

Nothing. She squints harder.

Daisy: Come on… you did it yesterday. Don’t embarrass me in front of my parents.

Jax gives a tiny shrug.

Jax: No shame in it not working on command, kiddo. Maybe it—

Daisy: Wait!

The crate twitches.

Daisy: There! Did you see that?

Jax: I… think so, yeah.

Daisy steps closer.

Daisy: Okay. So it can work. I just have to… find the right feeling.

She closes her eyes and puts her hand to her chest.

The crate jitters. The corners briefly sink into the ground.

Pomni flinches.

Pomni: Okay, that’s… that’s enough.

Daisy: No, this is good. I can actually do it.

She pushes a little harder.

The floor under the crate ripples.

Pomni: Daisy, slow down.

Daisy: I’ve got it.

Her heart’s racing now.

Daisy: I can make it move, so I can learn to make it stop. Isn’t that the whole point?

Pomni: Dee, listen to me. You’re already shaking. You need to—

Daisy: I’m fine!

The word comes out sharper than she meant.

The crate blurs and appears a few steps to the side, then back. The floor softens beneath Daisy’s feet. Pomni reaches for her arm.

Pomni: Daisy!

Jax: This is too much. Dial it back, Dee.

Daisy: I’m trying!

Her hands are shaking. The more they sound scared, the more something in her spikes.

Daisy: I’m trying. No. No. Stop!

She squeezes her eyes shut.

Daisy: Okay. Stop pushing. Stop… pushing.

She forces a breath in, then out. Tries to think small, boring thoughts.

The floor steadies under her feet.

Daisy opens one eye. Everything seems… normal. Mostly. She lets out a shaky breath.

Daisy: Okay. I think I… I stopped it.

Pomni still grips her arm, tight enough to hurt.

Pomni: You did… you pulled it back.

Daisy: Sorry.

Pomni: Don’t be, just… that’s too far.

Daisy: Yeah. I… yeah. I did it. I stopped.

Jax: Sure did, kiddo.

Daisy: Do you… think he noticed?

Pomni: I don’t know… I hope not.

Jax: With the amount of nonsense he throws at us, maybe he’ll just blame it on something else.

Daisy hugs herself.

Daisy: Yeah. Maybe.

She doesn’t believe herself.

Pomni: Come on. That’s enough science for today.

They walk back to Daisy’s room.

Daisy drops into her bed. Pomni pulls a chair and Jax leans back against the door.

Daisy: So, that was… a lot.

Jax: That’s an understatement.

Pomni: You pulled it back. That matters. Next time we stop way earlier. Little nudges, not… whatever that was.

Daisy: There’s gonna be a next time?

Pomni hesitates, then nods.

Pomni: We can’t ignore it. We can’t pretend it isn’t real. Next time, we do it slower. Safer.

Daisy: Okay. Next time… smaller.

Jax: We’re all in one piece, the tent’s still standing, and Caine hasn’t barged in yet, Pom. I’m calling this a success!

Pomni: I’m calling it we barely got away with it.

Daisy lets out a soft breath.

Daisy: Yeah. For today.

Caine pops up near the site of Daisy’s outburst.

Caine: ALRIGHTY… let’s figure out what caused the big wobble here earlier.

He floats down to the ground and takes a few steps.

Caine: Could’ve been just a random anomaly. Or maybe someone’s messing with the—

He pauses and stares at his cane, planted onto the floor, where Daisy was.

Caine: Oh.
